# Third-party components

The RACO browser app uses three CGRA-Flow parameter-model files under
`vendor/cgra_flow/common/`, copied from CGRA-Flow commit
`69d1929d683dd99843a58162c3f40101689a1c01`.
CGRA-Flow states BSD-3-Clause licensing in its README, retained as
`vendor/cgra_flow/UPSTREAM_README.md`.

HARP frontend components from the combined development workspace were omitted
from this RACO release. The browser does not embed the
CGRA-Flow desktop application.
