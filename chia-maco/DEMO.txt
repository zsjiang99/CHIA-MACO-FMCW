MACO × CHIA / FMCW: a three-minute artifact walkthrough

Setup: follow the repository README to install the evaluator and GUI dependencies,
build the frontend, and start `bash gui/start.sh`. Open http://127.0.0.1:8765/.
The landing page is the frozen seed-37 paper experiment. Reading it does not
start a model, mapper, synthesis, or physical-design job.

1. Read "MACO Agent Decisions". In round 1, the Fine Judge preferred a 6×6
   array, but actual mapper-derived frame costs favored the other shortlisted
   4×4 design (40.17 million estimated cycles). Round 2 repeated its plans:
   the cumulative count stayed at 10 unique mappings. The new 4×4 compiler
   setting in round 3 reached 39.15 million estimated cycles after 18 mappings.

2. Read "Agent vs. Reference". The agent reached the exhaustive reference's
   best estimate with 18 rather than 36 unique mappings, but used 210.43 s
   versus 38.71 s. This single-seed example is not a budget-matched search
   efficiency study.

3. Click Window, FFT, Transpose, Power, and CA-CFAR under Workload. Inspect
   each data-flow graph, mapping cycle, and raw Evidence log. The CA-CFAR
   neighborhood loop contributes 89.7% of the modeled best-frame cycles.

4. Read "Evidence & limits" and download the archived trace and reference.
   Native C vs NumPy strict CFAR masks agree in 2/6 scenes. Mapping II and
   an analytical cost model do not establish full-frame CGRA correctness,
   clock frequency, measured FPS, full-chip area, or total energy.

For a fresh agent/mapper experiment, select "Live exploration" in the header.
The live mode needs a configured model service and Docker mapper image. The
RTL/synthesis/layout buttons in that mode are experimental extensions outside
the paper's array/unroll comparison. They require separate setup and results
must not be described as whole-frame candidate hardware validation.

The GUI listens on localhost only; forward port 8765 via SSH for remote access.
Use `make -C chia-maco artifact-check` to verify the saved paper numbers and
the native smoke test without running the mapper or model.
